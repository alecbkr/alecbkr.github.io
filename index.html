Hey, my name's Alec

I'm a Software Engineer with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and experience building systematic components for large, real-time applications. I value my ability to problem solve with logicality and forward thinking so that my solutions are efficient and malleable for future developments.

PRISM - SHADER SANDBOX

A GLSL shader development environment built from scratch using a custom OpenGL-based engine.

Prism provides a user-friendly 3D workflow by abstracting low-level rendering and resource management while preserving direct access to OpenGL's programmable pipeline. It integrates a real-time 3D viewport, code editor, console, and asset management tools into a unified workflow.

SIMPLETON CHESS

A lightweight console chess game that demonstrates efficient game-state management and move validation in a text-based environment.

Simpleton Chess draws a custom ASCII board, enforces the rules of chess through legal move checking, and improves player interaction with visual indicators that highlight every valid move available to the selected piece.

SHUZZY

A web-based fishing companion that combines catch tracking with social features centered around user-shared fishing achievements.

Google Maps integration is enhanced with information about reported catches in local bodies of water. Users can log and track their catches over time while exploring the achievements and experiences of other local anglers.

OLD FAITHFUL VR SIM

An educational virtual reality experience showcasing the Old Faithful geyser in Yellowstone National Park, built in Unreal Engine.

Students are immersed in a virtual Yellowstone while a friendly tour guide narrates the park's history and facts about the Old Faithful geyser as they experience it errupt.
