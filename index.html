PRISM - SHADER SANDBOX

A GLSL shader development environment built on a custom OpenGL rendering engine.

Prism is a shader development environment that abstracts rendering and resource management while preserving direct access to OpenGL's programmable pipeline. It integrates a real-time 3D viewport, code editor, console, and asset management tools into a unified workflow.

SIMPLETON CHESS

Simpleton Chess is a lightweight console chess game that demonstrates efficient game-state management and move validation in a text-based environment. The game draws a custom ASCII board, enforces the rules of chess through legal move checking, and improves player interaction with visual indicators that highlight every valid move available to the selected piece.

SHUZZY

A web-based fishing companion that combines catch tracking with social features centered around user-shared fishing achievements. Google Maps is integrated and enhanced with information about reported catches in local bodies of water. Users can log and track their catches over time while exploring the achievements and experiences of other local anglers.
